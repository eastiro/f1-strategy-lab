## Pre-Registered Scope

Every conventional 2020 Race session was scanned under the unchanged V2.4
FastF1 protocol. No race was excluded.

The timing-laps-unavailable and weather-unavailable sessions are explicitly

## Interpretation

The retrievable 2020 sessions add no V2.4 candidate. This is an audit result,
not evidence that 2020 had no wet racing. The next historical scan must retain
the same state-machine rule and preserve unavailable-session counts alongside
any candidate result.
